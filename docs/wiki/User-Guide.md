# User Guide

Features as described in the README. Several are still being finished; check [BUILD_STATUS](../BUILD_STATUS.md) for what is implemented and validated.

- **Chop and generate.** Detect or place chops, then generate deterministic variations. Locks, roles and rules keep parts you like.
- **Recursive Hit Zoom.** Open a hit into a nested rhythm, up to three levels deep.
- **Fractal Rhythm.** Enter a motif such as `x.xx` and apply it at every scale.
- **Evolve.** The beat mutates while it plays. Locked bars and your edits stay put.
- **Smart Setup.** Suggests chops, roles, bars and tempo from the audio. Nothing is applied without confirmation.
- **Orbit View.** The phrase drawn as concentric rings, one per chop.
- **Per-hit effects.** State-variable filter, tape glide and bit/sample-rate crunch.
- **Export.** WAV (16, 24 or 32-bit float, up to 192 kHz) and a Producer Kit with each chop as a WAV plus a MIDI file.
- **Variation family tree.** Each Generate, Mutate and Zoom is kept as a branch you can return to.
